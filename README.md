# Justab 

By simply taking a photo, Justab recognizes all of the items on your receipt and gives your group the opportunity to fairly pay with ease. This is done alongside the ability to add/delete specific items, include a tip based on percent and an easily understandable party system. 


# Inspiration

After going to Dave & Busters with a large group of friends, one of our founders came up with the idea for Justab after having a difficult time determining who owes what. As multiple appetizers were split between many people on top of everyones main meal, the process easily went beyond simple math. Automating and simplifying this experience is why we designed Justab.  

Basically, while a simple split between 2 people for a dessert might not need any complex math, Justab is designed for work outings and other 20+ person gatherings when the bill isn't taken care of by one person.


# Overview (How It Works)

Justab is a receipt scanner which uses an OCR model on an app-based photo to analyze the receipt itself. Justab then does the math for you and clearly displays all of the critical information your group needs, all with the click of a few buttons. 

While our scanner is highly accurate, mistakes from bad quality or lighting in photos can potentially cause errors, which is why extra features like manual item changing and a review screen play an important role in the app. 

Once the items are reviewed and everything is entered, all users have to do is select whatever items they are paying for (even if another user has also claimed an item) and then view the final cost of what they owe.  

But everything then comes together when your group joins a party. Only one member is needed to manage a party, and joining can easily be done with a simple 6 digit code. Leaders of the party will be in control of adding and removing items, ensuring item correctness and tip percent, and starting the splitting process when everyone is ready. Members of the party just need to enter their name and a code, and then can get right to splitting.
